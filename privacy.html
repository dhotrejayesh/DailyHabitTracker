Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 1, 2026

Welcome to Daily Habit Tracker (https://dhotrejayesh.github.io/DailyHabitTracker/). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our web application.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Daily Habit Tracker, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Google Account Information: If you sign in using Google OAuth, we receive basic profile details authorized by you, such as your name, email address, and profile picture.
  • Habit & Activity Data: The habit names, frequencies, completion streaks, and goal logs that you create and track inside the application.
  • Local Storage Data: We may store your preferences and habit entries in your browser's local storage for responsive, offline-ready tracking.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to provide, maintain, and improve the application's functionality, including:

  • Authenticating your account and maintaining your active session.
  • Saving, synchronizing, and displaying your personal habits and progress charts.
  • Ensuring the technical reliability and security of the application.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

Daily Habit Tracker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your Google user data to any third party.
  • We do not use your Google user data for serving personalized advertising or marketing.
  • We do not transfer your data to third parties unless necessary to provide or improve the core features of the application, comply with applicable laws, or as part of a merger/acquisition with your consent.
  • Human workers do not read your user data unless you have given explicit consent for troubleshooting, it is necessary for security purposes, or it is required to comply with law.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your habit data is stored securely. For client-side functionality, data is retained within your local browser storage. Any cloud-synced information is transmitted over secure HTTPS encryption to protect against unauthorized access or disclosure.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • Local Data Deletion: You can clear your tracked habits and preferences at any time by clearing your browser cache/local storage or using the in-app reset controls.
  • Revoke Google Access: You can revoke Daily Habit Tracker's access to your Google Account at any time through your Google Security Settings.
  • Account Deletion Requests: If you wish to have all stored data associated with your account permanently deleted, please contact us using the information below.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly to this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
